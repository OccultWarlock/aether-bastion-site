Privacy Policy

Publisher: NoffTech. Last updated 27 August 2026.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for tracking or advertising. Account and identifiers Game Center aliases and player identifiers operate cohorts, co-op rifts, blocks, and sealed dispatches. Identifiers are stored so those features work. They are not shown in the interface. iCloud Your save lives in your private iCloud (CloudKit) container when iCloud Sync is on. Public CloudKit holds cohort listings you choose to join or found. Dispatches are HPKE-sealed; plaintext mail stays in your private iCloud. Purchases StoreKit processes gem packs, permanent packs, and Bastion Circle. Apple handles payment. We keep entitlements needed to grant what you bought. Player text Cohort names, notices, and dispatches are screened, can be reported, and can be blocked. Reports include a short snippet sent to safety@aetherbastion.app. Account deletion Settings → Delete Account removes this device’s save, your private CloudKit profile and mailbox, your public PlayerIndex, cohort membership, and dispatches you sent. Apple ID subscriptions are managed separately in Account Settings. Contact safety@aetherbastion.app support@nofftech.com
