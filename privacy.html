Aether Bastion · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Publisher: NoffTech LLC / NoffTech. Last updated 6 September 2026.

This policy covers Aether Bastion on the Apple platforms the game ships on: iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple TV. A visionOS build may follow; the same practices would apply if we ship one.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for tracking or advertising.

Account and identifiers

Game Center aliases and player IDs operate cohorts, co-op rifts, blocks, and sealed dispatches. Aliases appear in cohort and co-op UI. Player IDs are stored so those features work and are not shown in the interface.

iCloud

Your save lives in your private iCloud (CloudKit) container when iCloud Sync is on. Public CloudKit holds cohort listings you choose to join or found. Dispatches are HPKE-sealed. Plaintext mail stays in your private iCloud.

Purchases

StoreKit processes gem packs, permanent packs, and Bastion Circle. Apple handles payment. We keep entitlements needed to grant what you bought.

Player text

Cohort names, notices, dispatches, and in-run pings are screened, can be reported, and can be blocked. Reports store a short snippet as a public CloudKit SafetyReport.

Delete Account

Settings → Delete Account removes this device’s save, your private CloudKit profile and mailbox without recreating them, your public PlayerIndex, cohort membership, join requests, rank grants, SafetyReports for saved Game Center IDs, and dispatches you sent. Apple ID subscriptions are managed separately in Account Settings.
